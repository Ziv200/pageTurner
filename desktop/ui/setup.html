Welcome to PageTurner

Your Mac sends MIDI Program Changes (e.g. from Ableton) → PageTurner shows the right sheet-music page on your iPad. PageTurner lives in the menu bar (the page icon at the top right of the screen).

2MIDI input

To send Program Changes from Ableton on this Mac, turn on the built-in IAC Driver once:
  1. Click Open Audio MIDI Setup below, then in its menu choose Window › Show MIDI Studio.
  2. Double-click IAC Driver, tick “Device is online”, click Apply.
  3. In Ableton: Settings › Link, Tempo & MIDI – turn on Track for output “IAC Driver Bus 1”, then set a clip’s Pgm number and send the MIDI to the IAC bus.
